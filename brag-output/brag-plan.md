# Brag Plan: [CODE WITH GANDHI]

## What is this app?
[CODE WITH GANDHI] is an ultra-luxury, 3D-interactive single-origin coffee cafe and digital roastery sanctuary featuring real-time procedural 3D cups, steam dynamics, interactive drink customizers, and seamless digital membership.

## The angle
Coffee isn't commercial fuel — it's modernist architecture. We contrast generic beige coffee culture with an obsessive dark luxury aesthetic: 2,200m volcanic cloud forests, bespoke Italian spring-lever machines pulling shots at 9.2 bars, and real-time 3D drink formulations.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Giant cinematic display typography emerges from espresso darkness: "BREW BEYOND ORDINARY." The signature 3D ceramic cup tilts with glowing latte art, floating roasted beans, and rising steam particles.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The 3D Switcher**: Rapid swap of handcrafted drinks (Silk Velvet Flat White, Madagascar Cinnamon, Nitro Cold Brew) with morphing background typography (`VELVET`, `CINNAMON`, `NITRO`).
- **Tactile Formulation**: Live customization interface showing Size Grande, Oat Milk Emulsion, and +9.2 bar Ristretto Shot dynamically updating the live price.
- **The Terroir Pedigree**: 2,200m volcanic cloud forest origins and 92+ cup score badge.

## Outro / punchline
The Gold Patron digital member card with metallic gold foil and QR code locks into place. A burst of gold confetti confirms order `#CWG-ORD-9821`.
Final title: "[CODE WITH GANDHI] — Coffee as Architecture."

## User flow worth showing
Entry at 3D Hero → Interactive Barista Bar switching models → Customizing Size L + Extra Ristretto Shot → Gold Member Order Confirmation with celebratory gold confetti.
